FTP Client by YuvaByte Technologies

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 5, 2026

YuvaByte Technologies ("we", "our", or "us") built the FTP Client app. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All data stays on your device.

The app stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Server connection details — Hostname, port, username, and password for your FTP/SFTP/FTPS servers. These are stored in a local SQLite database on your device.
  • Transfer history — Records of file uploads and downloads, stored locally for your reference.
  • App preferences — Theme settings, accent color, default ports, and other configuration choices.

Credential Storage

Server passwords are stored locally using Flutter Secure Storage, which uses Android's EncryptedSharedPreferences (AES-256 encryption). Your credentials never leave your device and are never transmitted to us or any third party.

Network Activity

The app connects only to the servers you explicitly configure. We do not operate any backend servers. Network connections are made solely for:

  • Connecting to your FTP, SFTP, or FTPS servers
  • Browsing, uploading, and downloading files
  • Performing file management operations on your servers

No analytics, tracking, advertising, or telemetry data is sent anywhere.

Permissions

The app requests the following Android permissions:

  • Internet — To connect to your configured servers.
  • Storage — To download files to and upload files from your device.
  • Notifications — To show file transfer progress and completion alerts.
  • Biometric / Fingerprint — For the optional app lock feature. Biometric data is handled entirely by Android's system and is never accessed by the app.

Third-Party Services

This app does not use any third-party services such as:

  • Google Analytics or Firebase
  • Advertising networks
  • Crash reporting services
  • Social media SDKs

Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously. Server credentials are encrypted using AES-256 via Android's secure storage. All SFTP connections use SSH encryption. FTPS connections use SSL/TLS encryption. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. You are advised to review this page periodically.
